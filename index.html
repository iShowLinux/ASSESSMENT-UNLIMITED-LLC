Why Assessments Unlimited

Two decades of care,

Assessments Unlimited, LLC is a Woman-Owned Small Business founded in 2004 in Columbia, South Carolina — providing primary care, veteran evaluations, and wellness services with discretion and clinical precision.

Our Mission

To provide succinct, customized strategies for health and wellness — delivering optimal medical outcomes and expanding access and equity for individuals, families, and communities.
